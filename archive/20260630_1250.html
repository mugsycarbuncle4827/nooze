Chief Justice Roberts authored the opinion, which represents a landmark victory for the "unitary executive theory" that holds the president must have full control over all executive branch policymaking. Critics note the ruling is paradoxically at odds with the originalist principles the conservative court majority typically champions. The decision dramatically reshapes the balance of power between the presidency and Congress-created independent bodies.
