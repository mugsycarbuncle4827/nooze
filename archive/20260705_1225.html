Supreme Court Ends Independence of Federal Agencies in Trump v. Slaughter Ruling

The Supreme Court ruled in *Trump v. Slaughter* that while independent federal agencies like the FTC can continue to exist, the president now has broad authority to remove their members at will. The case arose when Trump fired Biden-appointed FTC commissioner Rebecca Slaughter, who sued to recover her position citing statutory protections. The decision effectively strips these agencies of the independence that had defined them, consolidating greater executive control over regulatory bodies.
